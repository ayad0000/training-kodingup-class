Pip and the Lost Little Star
Over the mossy hill, beyond the sleepy trees, there is a little place called Moonpetal Wood.

A place for small friends… and wonderful adventures.

Tonight, Pip the dragon and Willow the fairy are about to meet someone very far from home.

In Moonpetal Wood, the flowers were folding their petals for bed.

Bramble was putting away his berry basket.

And Willow was counting the first evening stars.

Willow: “One… two… three…”

Then something went… plink! …in the soft grass.

It was a little star.

No bigger than an acorn.

Its golden light blinked like a sleepy firefly.

Little Star: “Oh. I was peeking at your lovely wood. I leaned a little too far.”

Pip crouched beside it.

Pip: “Don’t worry. I’ll take you home!”

He spread his small wings.

Flapped once…

Flapped twice…

And landed with a gentle bump in a fern.

Pip: “My wings aren’t big enough.”

Willow sat beside him.

Willow: “You don’t have to do everything on your own.”

Bramble shuffled over.

Bramble: “Let’s think together.”

They tried a tall mushroom.

Too short.

They tried the hill.

Still too short.

Then Willow noticed something.

Willow: “Little star… when you laugh, your light gets brighter!”

Pip made his funniest face.

Bramble rolled into a wobbly ball.

And Willow did a tiny upside-down dance.

The star giggled.

Its light grew warm… and golden.

High above, another star blinked back.

Little Star: “My family!”

A silver moonbeam stretched down through the trees, making a shining path.

The little star floated up it… slowly… and safely.

Little Star: “Thank you for helping me shine!”

Pip watched until the star was a bright dot in the sky.

Pip: “I couldn’t carry it. But we still helped it home.”

Willow: “Together.”

Bramble: “Now… does helping a star count as a reason for one last berry?”

They all thought it did.

And above Moonpetal Wood, one little star twinkled an especially happy goodnight.

Sometimes, we can’t do something all by ourselves.

But we can ask a friend… and try together.

What could you do when something feels too hard?

Goodnight, little dreamer.

Another adventure will be waiting in Moonpetal Wood.
